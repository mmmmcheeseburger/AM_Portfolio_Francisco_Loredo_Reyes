My Future Plan

This is the path I plan to take after high school, combining military service, real-world experience, and college education to build my future.

Step 1

Join the Military in Intelligence

After graduation, I plan to enter the military and work in the intelligence field. My goal is to build discipline, leadership, responsibility, and gain experience in a career that requires focus, strategy, and critical thinking.

Step 2

Complete MOS Training

After entering the military, I plan to complete my MOS training and prepare for the specific responsibilities of my job. This stage will help me strengthen my technical knowledge, job readiness, and overall skill set.

Step 3

Deployment and Active Service

Once fully trained, I plan to serve, be deployed when needed, and carry out my job with professionalism. During this time, I want to gain real-world experience, stay committed to my responsibilities, and continue growing personally and professionally.

Step 4

Attend College While Serving

While completing my contract and doing my job, I plan to attend college at either Oakland University or Michigan State University. I want to continue my education while serving so I can keep progressing academically and build a strong future for myself.

Step 5

Build My Long-Term Career

My long-term goal is to use both my military experience and university education to build a strong career in a field connected to intelligence, technology, cybersecurity, or leadership. I want to create a future that is stable, meaningful, and built on discipline and growth.
